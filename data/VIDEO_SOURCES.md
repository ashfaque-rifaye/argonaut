# Argonaut test video library

Short clips of real rotating machinery and vibrating structures for testing Argonaut (tracking, sub-pixel vibration, order tracking / Campbell, OMA, cavitation audio).

- Clips: `data/raw/clips/<slug>.mp4` (H.264 + AAC, original fps, `+faststart`). Full sources: `data/raw/videos/<source>/` (gitignored, includes yt-dlp `source.info.json`).
- Machine-readable manifest: `data/raw/clips/manifest.json`.
- **Licensing:** each clip's licence was checked before download: YouTube `license` field = *Creative Commons Attribution license (reuse allowed)* (CC BY 3.0), or Wikimedia Commons CC0 / CC BY 3.0 / Public Domain. **CC BY clips need attribution** (author + source URL) wherever we show them (demo video, README, slides). Caveat: YouTube CC BY is the uploader's own declaration.

## Best picks by use case

- **Run-up / coast-down (order tracking, Campbell):** `fan-exhaust-84in-runup` (cleanest audio sweep), `fan-condenser-coastdown` (~40 s visual + audio coast-down), `rotor-kit-overview-unesp` (short coast-down at end), `pump-firepump-vertical-startup` (audio start-up).
- **Pump / cavitation audio:** `pump-submersible-air-slurp` (periodic air-ingestion bursts), `pump-intake-station-audio` (healthy pump/flow baseline), `pump-gear-cavitation-visual` (visual cavitation, no audio), `pump-brewing-airlock`.
- **Rotor kit / imbalance / resonance:** `rotor-kit-disk-closeup-unesp`, `rotor-kit-overview-unesp`, `resonance-coffee-cup`, `motor-magnet-enoise-eomys`, `bridge-tacoma-narrows-1940` (structural mode).

## Gaps (not found under a reusable licence)

- A centrifugal pump going into **true cavitation with clear audio** (healthy -> cavitating on the same pump). Best legal options: MIMII dataset audio (CC BY-SA 4.0), or record our own (throttle the suction on a small centrifugal/garden pump).
- A **machinery fault simulator** with deliberate misalignment/imbalance, and a **Jeffcott rotor passing its critical speed**: only standard-licence videos exist (listed below). Recommend recording our own on a tripod (e.g. a drill or bench grinder with an added unbalance mass, or a fan on a VFD/speed controller).
- **Slow-motion (high-fps) machinery vibration**: none found under CC. Highest fps here is 60 (`wind-turbine-brooklyn-vertical`) and 50 (`pump-gear-cavitation-visual`, `pump-intake-station-audio`).
- The gearbox, conveyor, compressor and cooling-tower fan clips found under CC were promotional, animated, handheld or low quality, so they were skipped. Pexels had no relevant industrial pump/fan footage.
